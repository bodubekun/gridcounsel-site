What comes back to you

What we review

Most of these agreements outlast the project's first owner, and every one of them gets read again in diligence.

Option and lease agreements
Option terms and extensions, rent and escalators, decommissioning security, assignment to financing parties.
Easements and crossings
Access, collection line and gen-tie easements, plus pipeline, rail and utility crossings.
NDAs and exclusivity letters
Mutual and one-way NDAs, LOIs, exclusivity periods and the rights to walk away.
Consultant MSAs and SOWs
Environmental, survey, geotech and interconnection consultants. Scope, IP and liability caps.
Estoppels and SNDAs
Landowner and lender deliverables that need to be clean before financing closes.
Road use and host agreements
County road use, good neighbor and community benefit agreements.
